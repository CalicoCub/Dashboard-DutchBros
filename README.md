# Dutch Bros Financial Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Dutch Bros Inc.'s financial and operational performance from 2020 through 2025. The analysis examines the relationship between the company's rapid store expansion, revenue growth, profitability, and operating efficiency.

The project was completed as part of an Intro to Data Analytics course at Ivy Tech Community College.

## Objective

The primary question for this analysis was:

> Should Dutch Bros continue prioritizing rapid store expansion, or should management focus more on improving profitability at existing locations?

The analysis evaluates this question by examining financial and operational trends across the six-year period.

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Data visualization
- Financial ratio and trend analysis

## Analysis

The project examines several financial and operational metrics, including:

- Revenue growth
- Store growth
- Revenue per store
- Operating margin
- Net profit margin
- Operating expense ratio
- Same-shop sales growth
- Company-operated vs. franchised locations

Additional year-over-year calculations and performance metrics were calculated in Excel.

## Dashboard

The project includes an Excel dashboard presenting key financial and operational trends through tables, charts, and visualizations.

## Key Findings

The analysis found that Dutch Bros experienced substantial growth between 2020 and 2025:

- Revenue increased from approximately $327 million to $1.64 billion.
- Store count increased from 441 to 1,136 locations.
- Revenue per store increased from approximately $742,000 to $1.44 million.
- Net profit margin improved from negative margins in 2021 and 2022 to 7.2% in 2025.
- Same-shop sales growth remained positive from 2021 through 2025.

These findings were used to evaluate the relationship between continued expansion and improving financial performance.

## Deliverables

- **Excel Dashboard** — Interactive financial and operational analysis
- **Financial Analysis Report** — Written analysis of findings and recommendations

## Data Sources

Financial and operational data were collected from Dutch Bros Inc. annual reports covering 2020–2025.

Additional information regarding future expansion plans and financial guidance was obtained from publicly available company announcements and news coverage.
